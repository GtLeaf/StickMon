# OSTB 3ST migration requirements

Status: implementation on codex/ostb-3st-adaptation; native game built and flashed. GPIO1 active-high middle key confirmed: short press locks/unlocks, long press powers off/on through the hardware controller. Snapshot protection, held-key pause and position restoration passed the final device check; save/startup logs match at world position (132.00,123.00).

## Goal

Run StickMon on the verified OSTB 3ST device using its full 296x240 landscape screen and physical buttons. Preserve the original pixel presentation and gameplay flows, with layouts adapted to the larger canvas. Use ESP-IDF 5.5.x and the existing custom RGB565 rendering approach without LVGL.

## Verified hardware baseline

- ESP32-S3, 16 MiB Flash, 8 MiB PSRAM; PSRAM memory test passed.
- Display: NV3023; 296x240 landscape, offset (24,0), MADCTL 0xe8; RGB565, SPI3 40 MHz.
- Display pins: MOSI10, CLK9, CS14, DC8, RESET18, backlight13.
- Static image direction, RGB order and visible window confirmed by two device photos and user feedback.
- Two full 16 MiB factory Flash reads are byte-identical and SHA256 verified. The backup remains outside the Git repository.
- Physical A=GPIO40 and B=GPIO39 confirmed by user button presses. The factory 2.2.2 firmware configures the middle key on GPIO1, active high, without internal pulls; this differs from the public reference's GPIO0.
- Audio, battery sensing, touch, motion sensors and sleep/wake behavior are not verified by the display test.

## Acceptance requirements

1. When the device starts, the game shall draw directly into a 296x240 RGB565 canvas. Menus, text and corner controls shall remain within the panel's rounded visible area. From application startup, the board shall keep the backlight off and defer panel display-on until the first complete frame has finished transferring; loading saved brightness shall not reveal uninitialized panel memory.
2. When a scene is shown, the game shall lay out its room, menu, exploration or battle content for 296x240, without stretching the completed 240x135 screen image. Existing sprites, fonts and pack semantics shall remain reusable. The room shall use the same 1x world and sprite pixel scale as StickS3. The full 240x161 room shall be centered at (28,39) on the 296x240 canvas with black margins, without stretching or camera following. Actor/frame registration, shadows, food and eggs shall retain their StickS3 sizes and share the centered room origin. World layers shall be clipped to the room rectangle. The clock and one/two monster status panels shall use the top margin, and operation hints shall use the bottom margin; menus and other scenes retain the full native canvas. Simulation coordinates and save schema shall remain unchanged.
3. When the player performs an existing action, the OSTB build shall use shared gameplay services and produce the same logical GameState result as other targets.
4. When saving and loading, the OSTB build shall use the existing shared SaveCodec and save protection rules. A new partition layout may be board-specific, but logical save compatibility shall remain shared.
5. When a frame or dirty region is submitted, the display backend shall retain the source framebuffer until transfers finish and shall wait for DMA completion before reusing transfer memory.
6. While a scene is static, the game shall follow the existing RenderDemand scheduling contract and avoid continuous redraws.
7. When physical buttons are operated, the input backend shall debounce them and report press, release and long-press behavior consistently. Existing two-button actions shall remain available.
8. The user specifies middle-key short press locks/unlocks the screen, long press powers down. It must not act as a gameplay Back button. GPIO1 uses the factory electrical configuration. Each debounced press requests persistence before the hardware controller can cut power during a hold. While the key remains held, gameplay shall pause so the displayed position matches the snapshot; after power-on, the saved position shall be restored before normal movement resumes. Starting after hardware power-off requires a long press. Software deep sleep on a long release, if the hardware leaves the MCU powered, enables GPIO1 high-level wake and remains a separate acceptance item.
9. When a feature requires unavailable or unverified motion input, its migration shall define a button alternative before being considered complete.
10. When shared platform, layout or input code changes, existing StickS3 and applicable AMOLED builds and targeted host checks shall continue to pass. Hardware verification shall be recorded separately from compilation.
11. When a supported screen controller is present, the OSTB target shall offer screen-coordinate touch input alongside A/B, using the existing RGB565 renderer. The first touch delivery covers tapping the main monster, bowl, egg, menu rows and navigation controls; bath dragging remains a later feature.
12. When touch has no saved device calibration, the device shall show four corner targets and one independent center check. Only a nondegenerate fit with at most 12 pixels of residual error shall enable gameplay touch. Failed checks shall display retry guidance; a missed center tap shall allow retrying the center before discarding the fitted corners. The calibration shall be stored separately from the shared game snapshot. B shall skip setup; holding A+B at startup shall request recalibration.
13. When a pointer is released on its original target within 10 pixels of its starting point and within one second, the scene shall execute one existing action. Dragging, read failure, lock, power-button holds, physical button input and scene changes shall cancel the pending click. Modal dialogs shall block underlying rows.
14. While a legacy submenu is displayed, visible Back/Select/Confirm controls shall occupy a reserved bottom strip. The direct-touch main menu, bag, shop, room, food, settings and computer entry shall instead use the full screen and their own visible navigation/actions. Product taps shall preview a shop item, and purchasing shall require a separate action tap. Existing item-use and reset confirmations shall remain in force.
15. When the main menu is opened on a touch device, it shall reuse the AMOLED icon-grid renderer with columns computed from available width and minimum card size. OSTB shall show three 88x72 cards per row and all eight release entries on one screen, without bottom Select/Confirm controls. Pressing shall show feedback; releasing on the original card shall activate it once. Empty cells and gaps shall not activate entries. Dragging and cancellation shall not activate cards. AMOLED shall retain its current two-column geometry.
16. When the OSTB bag or shop is opened, items shall use an adaptive touch grid with a visible Back control and vertical dragging when content exceeds the viewport. Tapping an item shall open its detail view without consuming it or spending coins. Use/buy/sell shall require a distinct action control; the existing item-use confirmation shall remain. Physical A/B shall still select, open and return. Static grids and detail pages shall stop rendering until input or a real timed change occurs.
17. When the room, food, settings or computer entry page is opened on OSTB, it shall use the AMOLED visual style adapted to 296x240: a circular back control, right-aligned title, cards or rows, press feedback and direct release actions without a Select/Confirm footer. The room shall offer food and bath cards; food shall list all seven foods with stock and the selected food, with vertical scrolling. The computer entry shall retain its existing communication and contact-list destinations. Contact details and communication flows remain a later batch. A/B shall remain usable.
18. While settings is open, brightness and volume shall support bounded horizontal sliders, with vertical movement scrolling the list instead. Slider cancellation shall not generate a click. The existing speed, idle timeout, help and reset actions shall remain available. Help shall have visible page controls and reset shall require a separate confirmation page whose safe action is Cancel. Static pages shall park until input or a timed change. Layout and interactions shall have host coverage before an app-only update that preserves resources, calibration and saves.
19. When the OSTB team page is opened, members shall use AMOLED-style sprite/name/level/HP cards and a circular Back header without the Select/Confirm footer. Releasing a card shall open its action popup; the leader offers status/moves, other permanent members also offer First/Leave where existing context and rules permit. Tapping outside shall dismiss the popup without activating a member behind it. Drags, cancellation and interrupted input shall not perform an action. Egg viewing, A/B access and exploration return shall remain usable. Status and moves pages shall retain their current layout, input and navigation. Gameplay/save rules shall remain unchanged, and the deferred global corner-safe layout shall remain deferred.

## First playable acceptance

Boot, hatch, home care, menus, exploration, battle, rewards and save/restart shall form one complete playable loop on the native screen. The first delivery shall record real frame/transfer timings and button behavior. Audio, battery calibration, deep sleep and cross-device ESP-NOW verification follow as separate hardware acceptance work.
